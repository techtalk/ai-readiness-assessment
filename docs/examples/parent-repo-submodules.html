Portfolio regime

Portfolio regime

Federated
  • Five dimensions inherited in every subject; no inherited-unbound anywhere.
  • Each subject's CI reuses the root workflows and each AGENTS.md defers to the root file rather than restating it.
  • Gap spread 0.35 — narrow.

Split ceiling

Common weak — enablement backlog

Observability — weak in two of three subjects. The root harness supplies no observability scaffolding, so each submodule invents its own. Fixing it once at the root fixes it three times.

Locally weak — team backlogs

workers: Agent input, Work patterns, Agent composition, Testing. Real but local; they do not generalise.

What this estate should do next

Lift observability at the root, not in each submodule — the binding already works, so a change there propagates without asking three teams to act.

Leave the workers gaps to the workers team. Pulling them into an estate-wide programme would slow the fix and blur who owns it.

Do not chase levels. All three subjects are Coherent; nothing here is out of step with itself.

There is no overall score, grade or percentage in this report, and no averaged gap across subjects — averaging destroys the spread, which is the only thing a portfolio view adds. The headline is a distribution and a ceiling.
