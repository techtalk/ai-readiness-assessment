Portfolio regime

Portfolio regime

Fragmented — with lineage
  • No habitats declared; all 84 placements are local. That rules out Federated and Distributed.
  • Four services carry a HARNESS.md descended from a common ancestor — checkout, search, inventory, pricing — same section structure and rule names, diverged bodies.
  • pricing is an older copy missing the CI-gate section the other three have, which is most of why its gap is widest.
  • fulfilment wrote its own and shares nothing.
  • Moderate spread (0.93) separates this from Islanded — four copies drifting, not one team pulling ahead.

Split ceiling

Common weak — enablement backlog

Agent composition, Observability — weak in four of five active subjects. Neither appears in any copied harness, so every team was left to invent them and four did not.

Locally weak — team backlogs

pricing is behind across the board, and the lineage analysis says why: it runs an older copy of the shared ancestor. legacy-batch is excluded from common-weak by its maintenance posture — otherwise it would set the ceiling on eleven of fourteen dimensions.

What this estate should do next

Extract the common layer — and treat the divergences as the work. Four services maintain four copies of one harness. The extraction is not the hard part; reconciling what the copies disagree about is. Three Python versions and two coverage thresholds encode four local decisions — some real requirements, some accidents nobody remembers making. Go through them deliberately rather than picking the newest copy and declaring it canonical.

Start with pricing. Widest active gap, cheapest to close: it is not missing capability, it is missing the CI-gate section the other three copies already have.

Put agent composition and observability into the extracted layer, not into each service — they are weak in four of five precisely because no copy of the ancestor ever had them.

Leave fulfilment alone for now. It shares no lineage, it is the only subject whose habitat is ahead of the team, and folding it in would mean reconciling a fifth unrelated design for no benefit.
